Install notes — Mac
Open the DMG, drag PallettAi Studio into Applications, then open it. This build is code-signed but not notarized with Apple, so right-click the app in Applications → Open if macOS shows a security warning. Your projects live in the app's local storage — sign in to sync your plan and license across devices.
