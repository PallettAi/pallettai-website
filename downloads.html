v0.3.8 — What's new in Studio
Billing & plans
  • Pay for Pro (£9/mo) or Pro+ (£19/mo) in Studio. Stripe unlocks the plan on the account you signed in with — not a local demo switch.
  • After checkout, Studio waits and unlocks the plan when Stripe confirms. Coming back with ?paid=1 does the same.
  • Failed, canceled, or past-due payments show an honest back-on-Free status. License keys are left alone.
  • Plan & billing shows a real receipt: plan, source, renewal, and last billing event.
  • Local leftovers are gone — no simulate-a-friend redeem, no fake checkout unlock, no Switch to Free that fights Stripe.
Review gift
  • Leave one testimonial in Settings and get 3 days of Pro+. One claim per account; a second submit does nothing.
  • If you are already on paid Pro or Pro+, the review still saves and the claim is used, but no extra days are added.
  • Plan & billing now reads Pro+ Review Trial · 3 day, plus time left in hours and minutes (e.g. 71h 54m). The sidebar pill shows Pro+ · 71h 54m.
Account
  • Connects only to the official PallettAI registry — no pasted project URL or key.
  • Forgot password on sign-in; change password when signed in.
  • The signed-in Settings card is clearly a subscription login, not something that appears on client sites.
Studio
  • ⌘K / Ctrl+K command palette for views, billing, export, and the tour.
  • System theme (follows the OS), plus Dark and Light.
  • Sidebar and Settings tabs use stroke SVG icons instead of emoji.
  • Modals trap focus and close with Esc.
  • Export copy says the site is files you own, not a tenant.
  • Daily streak lives on AI Studio, not the dashboard.
  • Templates have their own view.

Install notes — Mac
Open the DMG, drag PallettAi Studio into Applications, then open it. This build is unsigned, so right-click the app in Applications → Open if macOS shows a security warning. Your projects live in the app's local storage — sign in to sync your plan and license across devices.
