Install notes — Mac
Open the DMG, drag PallettAi Studio into Applications, then open it. This build is unsigned, so right-click the app in Applications → Open if macOS shows a security warning. Your projects live in the app's local storage — sign in to sync your plan and license across devices.
v0.3.9 — What's new in Studio
AI Studio that listens
  • A structured brief — name, area, offer, three proofs, primary CTA, and a voice (warm, premium, or punchy) — now drives generation end to end, instead of only the prompt.
  • Voice lock keeps every generated line on-tone and strips banned phrases, so “premium” never drifts into hype.
  • Generation is fingerprint-seeded: the same brief rebuilds the same site every time, and a one-click Shuffle look changes palette, fonts, and hero without touching your copy.
Multi-page sites
  • A filled brief splits the site into real pages — Home, Menu, About, and Contact — instead of one long scroll. One-pagers still work when you ask for one.
  • Contact gains a map when the brief sets an area; the nav CTA matches the brief's call to action.
Twenty new niche packs
  • Plumber, electrician, solicitor, dentist, accountant, tutor, estate agent, landscaper, vet, physio, nursery, pub, wine bar, hotel, garage, tattoo studio, architect, locksmith, catering, and brewery — each with tailored sections, tables, and imagery cues.
  • Competitor study borrows structure only — service headings, never their copy. A filled brief always wins the words.
Copilot follow-ups
  • “Make the hero punchier”, “say the area more”, or “less salesy” rewrites the section you last touched — it remembers the target.
  • “Make it more like https://…”, “add a menu for a wine bar”, and “add a services page” are now real one-click actions.
Translate a finished site
  • French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, or Polish — every line of copy at once, brand name translated only if you tick the box.
  • Signed-in translates run on DeepL through the official registry; without an account, a free MyMemory fallback still works.
  • Exports carry the translated <html lang> and a “Translations powered by DeepL / MyMemory” footer.
Billing & polish
  • Manage billing opens the Stripe Customer Portal for signed-in subscribers — cancel or update the card without emailing anyone.
  • Every generated palette passes WCAG AA text contrast automatically.
