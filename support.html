Use a project enquiry for

  • New work — a new site, a redesign, or an upgrade that isn't part of a plan.
  • New bots and automations — anything that doesn't exist yet.
  • Quotes and proposals — scope, price, timeline, deposits.
  • Big changes — work that adds pages, features or integrations beyond your agreed scope.
  • New subscriptions — starting aftercare, or moving to a different tier.

If it's genuinely borderline, send it anyway. Forwarding it internally takes us seconds; asking you to re-send it wastes your day.

What to put in your email

A little detail up front usually means one reply instead of four. Where you can, include:

  • Your web address or bot name — exactly what's affected.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead — one sentence is fine.
  • When it started — today, after an update, since launch.
  • What you've already tried — if anything, so we don't repeat it.
  • A screenshot or a link — and any error message word for word.
  • The email you signed up with — so we can find your account and plan quickly.

Never send passwords, card numbers, or API keys. We don't need them, and we won't ask for them.

Questions

Support, answered straight.

If yours isn't here, ask it. A question that isn't in the FAQ is usually a sign the FAQ should change.

Is support free?
Yes, for anything we built and for anything you're paying us an aftercare plan for. Fixing a fault in our own code is on us — always. Work that's outside the original scope, or a plan you've cancelled, is quoted as a project. If you're not sure which side of the line something falls on, ask and we'll tell you before we start, never after.
I cancelled aftercare. Can I still get support?
Of course — you can always email us. What changes is scope: with a plan, changes and monitoring are covered; without one, we'll answer questions and quote any work that needs doing. Your build keeps the 30-day free fix window on our own code from launch regardless of plan.
My site or bot is down right now. What do I do?
Email support@pallettai.org with URGENT in the subject and the name of the site or bot. Outages get looked at immediately during working hours, ahead of everything else. If a third-party platform is down, we'll confirm that and tell you what we can and can't do about it.
I found a security problem. Where do I report it?
Email support@pallettai.org with Security in the subject. Include what you found and how to reproduce it, and nothing else needed — no exploits against live customer data, please. We'll acknowledge quickly, keep you updated, and credit you if you'd like.
How do I cancel or change my aftercare plan?
From the customer portal, or just email us and we'll do it. Monthly plans cancel any time with no charge beyond the current billing period. Prepaid annual plans are refunded pro-rata for unused months. There's no notice period and no awkward phone call.
What about my data, access requests, or complaints?
Same inbox — support@pallettai.org. Data access, correction and deletion requests are handled under our privacy policy (we respond within one month, usually much faster), and anything about quotes, deposits or disputes falls under our terms of service.
Do you support anything on the grader or the free checker?
Yes. If a check looks wrong, a site won't load in our tool, or you want the report explained, email us with the address you graded. The checker is free, and support for it is free too.
