Because our work is bespoke and starts as soon as you instruct us, the regulations allow the right to cancel to be lost where you expressly consent to work starting during the cooling-off period and acknowledge that you lose the right to cancel once it does. Your statutory rights are never affected. If you're a consumer, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 still protects you: services must be carried out with reasonable care and skill, and digital work must be as described and fit for purpose.
