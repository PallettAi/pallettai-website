0.3.8 Billing & Studio polish

Paid plans now unlock through Stripe on the signed-in account, and Studio itself is tidier to live in.

  • Pro (£9/mo) and Pro+ (£19/mo) checkout in Studio — Stripe unlocks the plan on the account you signed in with.
  • Failed, canceled, or past-due payments return you to Free. License keys are left alone.
  • One testimonial in Settings claims 3 days of Pro+. Already on a paid plan? The review saves; no extra days.
  • Official registry only — forgot / change password, command palette, system theme, SVG icons, Esc-safe modals.
