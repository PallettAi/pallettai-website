0.3.12 Color Lab & first-draft generate

Studio now looks like one instrument from the rail through QR and Settings, and a first draft arrives with better photos and a layout that fits the job.

08 Sep 20260.3.11

0.3.11 Critical fixes & signed builds

The hardened 0.3.10 build crashed at launch — 0.3.11 fixes it and makes auto-updates actually work.

  • Fixes a launch crash in the hardened build (browser-process V8 snapshot fuse turned off) — packaged apps start cleanly.
  • Fixes a blank window where the app could fail to load its own UI from the package (file:// privilege fuse back to its safe default).
  • Fixes the startup splash occasionally remaining pinned over the main window.
  • Restores deny-by-default permission handling (was registered on the wrong API).
  • Code-signed builds for Apple Silicon and Intel via a free self-signed certificate — no Apple Developer account required.
  • Auto-update feed restored: older installs update on launch, and new downloads stay current.

0.3.9 AI Studio upgrade

Generation now listens to a real brief — and can hand you a multi-page site, in seven languages.

  • Structured brief: name, area, offer, three proofs, CTA, and a voice (warm / premium / punchy) drive generation end to end.
  • Filled briefs split into multi-page sites (Home, Menu, About, Contact); voice lock keeps copy on-tone.
  • Twenty new niche packs — trades, professionals, hospitality — with tailored tables and sections.
  • Copilot follow-ups: “make the hero punchier”, “make it more like [URL]”, “add a services page”.
  • Translate any finished site via DeepL (signed-in) or MyMemory, with a powered-by footer in exports.
  • Fingerprint-seeded generation: same brief, same site — with a one-click Shuffle look that never rewrites copy.
  • Manage billing opens the Stripe Customer Portal for signed-in subscribers.
