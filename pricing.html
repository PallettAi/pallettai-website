Questions

Answered
straight.

If yours is not here, ask it — the answer will be plain either way.

How does a project start?
Send the form or an email describing the idea. We reply within 48 hours, agree a price and a small deposit, then build a preview you can look at. Nothing goes live until you are happy with it.
Can I pay online by card?
Yes — every payment runs through Stripe. New projects start with a deposit link (Refresh £49, Create Website £99, Custom Builds & Bots £149); the remaining balance is paid by a second link or invoice before launch. Aftercare is a monthly or annual subscription you can manage at any time in the customer portal.
What does aftercare actually cover?
The unglamorous work of keeping a site healthy: backups, security checks, small copy swaps and the occasional fix. Care and Care Plus add a monthly health report and we make the changes for you; Care Plus also includes monthly build time for new pages. It is optional — every site we build runs fine without it — but it is the easiest way to stay current and secure.
Do I own everything you build?
Yes. The code, the content and the domain are yours. If we ever part ways, you get a proper handover so nothing is locked behind us.
Can I switch tiers or cancel later?
Whenever you like. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel with 30 days' notice — no exit fees. Cancel during the free first month and you pay nothing. Once subscribed, the customer portal is where you change cards, view invoices or cancel in a couple of clicks.
Why "from £149" and "from £349"?
Because the work is not always the same size. A three-page refresh costs less than a booking flow. After a short conversation we quote a fixed price, and that number stays put.
