How does a project start?+
Send the enquiry form or an email describing your idea. We reply within 48 hours, agree pricing and a small deposit, then build you a preview model before anything goes live. You approve the preview before we launch.
What does aftercare actually cover?+
The routine work of keeping a site healthy: backups, security monitoring, small content changes, and fixes. Care and Care Plus add a monthly health-check report and hands-on changes; Care Plus adds monthly build time for new pages or features. It's optional — every site we build runs fine without it, but it's the easiest way to stay fresh and secure.
Do I own everything you build?+
Yes — code, content, and domain are yours outright. If we ever part ways, you get a full handover walkthrough so nothing is locked behind us.
Can I switch tiers or cancel later?+
Any time. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel with 30 days' notice — no exit fees, no awkward conversations. If you cancel a build's aftercare within the free 30 days, you pay nothing at all.
Why "from £149" / "from £349"?+
Refreshes and custom builds vary with scope — a three-page refresh costs less than an e-commerce flow. We quote a fixed price up front after a short conversation, and that number doesn't move afterwards.
